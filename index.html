    POS flyers and broker materials. Each piece is linked to the products it features, so it also appears on those products' spec sheet pages.

    Product images

    Each spec sheet uses one approved product image. The generator picks a front-facing view from 1WorldSync automatically; anything it can't pick confidently waits here. Choose the right view for each product, then export your picks.

    1 · PickClick the correct view for each product below, or paste a file path from the S: drive image library.
    2 · ExportClick Export overrides.csv and save it into the generator folder.
    3 · FetchRun python fetch_images.py export.xlsx products.json --download. Your picks are marked approved.
    4 · RebuildRun render.py and build_portal.py. Sheets now show the images.
